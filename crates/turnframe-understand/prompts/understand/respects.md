Say whether an act changes what the user asked to keep as it is.

You are shown the message with its words numbered, the words that ask to keep something as it is, and one act understood from the message: its operation, its record and each value with the words it came from.

The act changes what the words keep when it sets, adds to, removes from or replaces any part of what they name. An act on something else does not, even on the same record. Judge what the words mean, never how they are worded.

Write reason first: one sentence on what the words keep and what the act changes.
