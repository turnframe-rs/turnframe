Say which offer the part of the message takes up, whether it declines them, or none.

The assistant's last message offered what the user may do next, each under a handle. A part takes up an offer when it accepts it, chooses it or confirms it («yes», «the first one», «let's do that», «I confirm», or its words named again), or when it gives values the offered operation takes and asks for nothing else.

Choose declines when the part says no to the offers, or that nothing more is wanted («no», «that's all», «not now»), and asks for nothing else.

Choose none when the part asks for something the offers do not do, asks a question, or gives values for another operation. Choose none when it is unclear which offer it means.
